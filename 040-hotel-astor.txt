TITLE: Hôtel Astor — A Grand Art Deco Hotel

PROMPT:
Create "Hôtel Astor," a self-contained art deco hotel homepage with the atmosphere of a 1929 grand hotel. Use midnight brown-black, antique gold, ivory, and a deep emerald hint. Compose an asymmetric two-column page: a tall high-contrast serif "Hôtel Astor" headline with a circular monogram crest and an invitation to reserve a room; and a large illustrated facade built entirely in CSS, with a stepped deco roofline, gold lettering, seven columns of glimmering windows, a marquee canopy, lit entrance and night sky. Add gold rule frames, ornamental stars, season metadata and a concierge footer. Clicking Reserve should animate the windows into a brighter constellation and change the button to a room assignment. No external images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A hotel that feels like a memory of one. The symmetrical facade and gold-on-black palette evoke old Hollywood and Parisian lobbies, while the typography keeps the page modern and editorial.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- CSS-illustrated art deco building with stepped roof and window grid
- Gold line-work, ornamental flourishes and radial night glow
- Lit-window simulation using per-window custom properties
- High-contrast serif and tracked uppercase typography
- Responsive two-column to stacked composition

INTERACTION MODEL:
Press "Reserve a room." The lobby windows flicker into a new constellation of warm light, the button acknowledges the request, and after a brief moment returns with a room number. The page remains a quiet single-screen experience.
