TITLE: The Orrery — Six Planets, One Brass Machine

PROMPT:
Create "The Orrery," a self-contained mechanical solar system in midnight blue and brass. Compose a three-column observatory sheet: an editorial "The orrery" title with a list of six bodies (Mercury to Saturn) showing distance; a large inline-SVG orrery with concentric brass orbital rings, a graduated rim of 72 ticks, a glowing sun, six coloured planets and optional labels, all animated in JavaScript by angular velocity proportional to real orbital period; and a side panel with the selected body's name, rank, distance, period, diameter, moons, day length, orbit shape and description, plus a rate slider, pause and label toggles. Adjusting the slider should speed or slow every planet; clicking a planet or its list entry should select it. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
An Enlightenment instrument rather than a space poster. The brass rings and the slow, accurate relative motion make the page feel handled, and the data panel rewards the click without breaking the spell.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Inline-SVG concentric orbits with graduated brass rim
- JavaScript animation with real relative orbital periods
- Radial-gradient sun with a blurred glow layer
- Planet selection synced between drawing and index
- Brass-and-midnight instrument palette with engraved annotations

INTERACTION MODEL:
Drag the rate slider from 0× to 8× to change the speed of the machine. Press Pause or Resume, toggle planet labels, and click any planet or list entry to read its data and description.
