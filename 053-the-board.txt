TITLE: The Board — A Motherboard Atlas

PROMPT:
Create "The Board," a self-contained interactive motherboard atlas that treats a circuit board as a city map. Use deep PCB green, copper-gold traces, cream type, mint LED green and a red status light. Build a wide circuit board in CSS and inline SVG: golden trace roads with pads, a CPU square, RAM ridges, an I/O chip, a CMOS coin cell, PCIe slots and edge ports, plus capacitors, mounting pads and tiny status LEDs. Clicking any component highlights it and updates a component card with its city analogy. A power button toggles the board on, lights the LEDs and updates clock speed, temperature and power draw. Add editorial title, revision metadata and footer. No external assets, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
An engineering artefact read as urbanism. The dense gold traces and dark green solder mask give the page the beauty of infrastructure viewed from above, and the microcopy gives it wit without undercutting the craft.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Inline SVG trace network with vias and line-weight variation
- CSS-rendered chips, capacitors, slots, battery and edge ports
- Interactive component selection with glow states
- Power state that switches LEDs and live readouts
- Technical board labels and responsive recomposition

INTERACTION MODEL:
Click the CPU, RAM, I/O chip, CMOS battery, PCIe slots or ports to highlight them and read their city analogy. Press the power button to switch the board on or off; LEDs illuminate and the clock, temperature and wattage figures respond.
