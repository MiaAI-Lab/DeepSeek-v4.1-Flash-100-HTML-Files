TITLE: The Rialto — One Screen, Three Showings

PROMPT:
Create "The Rialto," a self-contained single-screen cinema booking page. Use deep velvet red, near-black, warm cream and brass. Compose a three-column programme: an editorial "The Rialto" title, a small illustrated film poster in inline SVG, film metadata and three showtime buttons with prices; a centred auditorium with a curved screen, six rows of ten seats split by an aisle, with sold, available and selected states, and a seat legend; and a box-office panel with chosen seats, ticket count, price, total, a buy button and a ticket stub. Selecting a show should reset availability to that performance; clicking seats should select or deselect them and update the total; buying should issue a booking code and disable further seat changes. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A box office with the hush of a small cinema. The seat map does the work, the brass accents keep it from feeling like a utility, and the ticket stub is the reward for committing.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- CSS seat map with rows, aisle and four seat states
- Curved screen and auditorium layout
- Per-showtime availability model with price changes
- Live total, seat list and booking-code generation
- Velvet, cream and brass cinema palette

INTERACTION MODEL:
Choose 17:30, 19:15 or 21:00 to change price and sold seats. Click available seats to select them; the seat list, ticket count and total update. Press Buy tickets to issue a six-character booking reference and lock the booking.
