TITLE: The Last Lighthouse — A Keeper’s Log in Three Weathers

PROMPT:
Create "The Last Lighthouse," a self-contained night scene and keeper's log. Use deep sea navy, warm lamp amber, cream lighthouse stone and signal red. Build a full-width coastal scene with a rotating searchlight beam, a glowing lantern room, a red-banded tower, gallery rail, rocks, sea and waves. Provide three weather buttons — Clear, Fog and Squall — that change sclass states to add fog haze, rain streaks and a darker sea, and replace the keeper's log entry and wind, sea, visibility and pressure readouts. A lamp button should extinguish or relight the beam and add a log entry about it. Keep the palette nocturnal, the typography period-appropriate and the lighthouse geometry clean. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A single object under changing weather. The beam is the animation and the log is the voice, and the weather buttons let the reader change both the scene and the keeper's state of mind.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Two rotating conic-light beams with clip-path shaping
- Layered CSS lighthouse with lantern glow, red bands and gallery
- Weather states altering fog, rain and sea gradients
- Log entries and readouts swapped per weather
- Night maritime palette with warm light discipline

INTERACTION MODEL:
Choose Clear, Fog or Squall to change the scene and the keeper's log, wind, sea state, visibility and pressure. Press Lamp on/off to extinguish or relight the beam and write a corresponding log entry.
