TITLE: The Aquarium — One Tank, Three Species

PROMPT:
Create "The Aquarium," a self-contained freshwater fish tank page. Use deep teal water, sandy aquarium gravel, harlequin amber, neon blue-green and cherry coral. Compose a three-column display: an editorial "The aquarium" title with a stock list; a glass tank with water gradient, gravel, swaying plants, rising bubbles and small CSS fish swimming at different depths; and a species panel with Latin name, note, origin, size, temperature, pH, shoal size and level, plus Feed the fish and Lights off controls and a log. Selecting a species should update the panel; feeding should add falling flakes, briefly speed the fish and write a log line; the light toggle should darken the tank and swap the log. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A quiet tank rather than an infographic. The water, bubbles and fish are all built from simple shapes, and the small controls make the page feel like something being kept alive.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Layered water gradient with glass highlight and gravel bed
- Animated fish silhouettes at different depths and speeds
- Rising bubbles and swaying plants
- Day/night tank state with dimmed fish
- Feeding particles and live log responses

INTERACTION MODEL:
Click a species to read its details. Feed the fish to drop flakes and quicken their swimming; toggle the lights to switch the tank between day and night. Every action writes a short line in the log.
