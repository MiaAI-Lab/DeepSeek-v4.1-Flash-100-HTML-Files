TITLE: Receipt — Everyday Poetry, Printed

PROMPT:
Create "Receipt," a self-contained whimsical poetry machine styled as a thermal receipt printer. Use warm counter-grey, bright receipt paper, near-black ink and a single distressed red stamp accent. Compose a playful three-column layout: a bold "Every receipt" headline and poetic description on the left, a centred illustrated printer with a paper receipt emerging from its slot in the middle, and a right-hand "choose a feeling" panel. The receipt must feel real: perforated bottom edge, dashed rules, monospaced SKU-like rows, barcode, issue number, faded stamp. Six moods — Tender, Restless, Grateful, Tired, Hopeful, Strange — each print a different small poem and update receipt metadata. A "Print another poem" button advances through them with a paper-feed animation. Everything inline and offline; no external assets, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive and valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A tiny poetry store as a piece of product theatre. The printer is charming but not cute, the paper is tactile, and each receipt treats a passing feeling with the gravity of a transaction.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- CSS printer body with inset shading, status LED and paper slot
- Perforated receipt paper with dashed separators and barcode
- Paper-feed animation on each new print
- Mood-based poem selection and metadata generation
- Warm paper-grain counter, rubber stamp and monospaced receipt typography

INTERACTION MODEL:
Choose a mood to print a poem matched to it; the receipt content, issue number, serial, stamp and line items change together and the paper feeds out of the printer. "Print another poem" cycles to the next feeling. Everything updates instantly without reloading.
