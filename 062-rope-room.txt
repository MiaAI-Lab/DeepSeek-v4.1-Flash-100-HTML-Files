TITLE: The Rope Room — Three Knots Worth Knowing

PROMPT:
Create "The Rope Room," a self-contained knot manual for three essential knots: the bowline, the clove hitch and the figure-eight. Use deep navy, warm rope tan, cream and a signal red accent. Compose a three-column plate: an editorial title and a three-knot index; a circular diagram plate with hand-drawn rope curves, shadows, twist dashes and numbered step dots; and an instructional panel with knot name, type, numbered steps, a knot-family fact grid, a strength-retained bar and an italic use note. Selecting a knot should swap the diagram, steps, facts, strength bar and quote. Clicking a step or its numbered dot should highlight both in sync. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A knot board that behaves like a patient instructor. The rope is drawn with enough weight and shadow to feel physical, and the numbered dots tie the drawing to the written steps.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Hand-authored inline-SVG rope paths with shadow underlay and twist dashes
- Circular diagram plate with dashed register ring
- Synced step selection between text list and diagram markers
- Knot data model driving all panels and the strength bar
- Nautical serif and engraved mono typography

INTERACTION MODEL:
Choose Bowline, Clove hitch or Figure-eight from the index to load that knot. Click any numbered step in the list or its matching dot on the diagram to highlight it. Facts, strength retained and the use note update with the selected knot.
