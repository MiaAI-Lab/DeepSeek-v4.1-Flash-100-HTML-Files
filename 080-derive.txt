TITLE: A Dérive in Nine Turns — A Walk with No Destination

PROMPT:
Create "A Dérive in Nine Turns," a self-contained psychogeographic walk generator. Use map paper, ink blue-grey, pale water, park green, cream streets and a single route red. Compose a three-column walk sheet: an editorial "A dérive" title, explanation and instruction card with Draw and Reset buttons; a hand-drawn street map with blocks, a river, crossing streets, a compass and a live route drawn as a red dashed polyline with numbered stops; and an itinerary that lists turns with times, plus distance, elapsed time and heading readouts. Each draw should select an instruction, choose a heading change, draw a new segment within the map bounds, add a numbered stop and update totals. Nine turns ends the walk. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A walk that is generated but not random-feeling: the deck supplies the poetry, the geometry supplies the shape, and the map accumulates a route that reads like a record of attention rather than a journey.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Procedural route drawing on an inline-SVG street plan
- Deck-driven instruction text with dated itinerary entries
- Bearing-based heading changes and bounded segment generation
- Route polyline, numbered markers and moving origin dot
- Map-paper palette and compass furniture

INTERACTION MODEL:
Press "Take a turn" to draw the next instruction, extend the route and record a stop with a time. Each turn updates distance, elapsed time and heading. After nine turns the walk ends. "Start again" clears the route and the itinerary.
