TITLE
PRISM — The Card That Shouldn't Exist

PROMPT
Create a holographic trading-card showcase called "PRISM". Deep blue-black room, faint grid and stars, one 300×430 collectible card floating at the centre with a rainbow foil surface, chrome orb artwork, tiny serial typography, stat bars and a holographic glare that follows the pointer. The card tilts in 3D as the cursor moves and flips to a matte back with specs, a barcode and a manifesto line. Around it, editorial fragments: "PRISM / the card that shouldn't exist", a rarity selector (Holo / Gold / Ice) that changes the foil palette, a Flip button, and small technical captions. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS, no external images or fonts.

DESIGN INTENT
Collectible culture treated as an art object. The card is the hero and must look expensive: foil that shifts, clean printing, a chrome planet rendered in gradients, immaculate microtype. The room around it is dark and instrumental, making the card feel lit from within. Rarity changes are subtle and material, not confetti.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
- CSS 3D tilt with pointer-driven rotateX/rotateY and a moving radial glare layer.
- Multi-stop holographic gradients with blend modes for rainbow foil; rarity variants swap the gradient palette.
- Chrome orb artwork built from radial gradients, ring ellipses and specular highlights.
- Front/back card faces with backface-visibility and a flip state.
- Microtype, stat bars, serial numbers, foil-stamped icon and a barcode made of repeating linear gradients.
- Faint star field, drafting grid, card shadow and ambient floor glow.
- Responsive scaling of the card and reflow of captions around it.

INTERACTION MODEL
Move the pointer to tilt the card and move its glare. "Flip card" turns it to the matte back and back again. Holo / Gold / Ice buttons change the foil palette and the colour of the ambient glow. The card can be focused and flipped with Enter. Reduced-motion replaces tilt with a static, perfectly lit card while keeping all states.
