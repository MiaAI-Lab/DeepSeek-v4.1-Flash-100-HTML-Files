TITLE: Small Things — Four Specimens Under the Lens

PROMPT:
Create "Small Things," a self-contained specimen cabinet for four tiny found objects. Use warm paper, sepia ink, moss green and rust, with a quiet naturalist's palette. Compose a three-column cabinet sheet: an editorial "Small things" title and four specimen buttons (feather, shell, dandelion seed, moth wing); a square specimen card with a hand-drawn inline-SVG object and a circular magnifying lens showing a zoomed detail; and a specimen record with Latin-ish subtitle, note, size, mass, place found, date, family, state and magnification. Selecting a specimen should redraw the object and lens, move the lens, and update every field. Clicking the lens or the magnification button should cycle the zoom and adjust the lens detail. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A cabinet of very small things, treated with the seriousness usually reserved for monuments. The lens is the interaction: it turns a delicate drawing into a study of structure.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Four hand-authored inline-SVG specimen drawings
- Circular magnifying lens with a scaled duplicate of the drawing
- Cycleable magnification and lens repositioning
- Specimen-sheet typography with field-record metadata
- Warm paper, sepia and moss palette

INTERACTION MODEL:
Choose Feather, Shell, Seed or Moth wing to load its drawing, lens position and record. Click the lens or the × button to cycle magnification from ×2 to ×8; the lens redraws its detail at the new scale.
