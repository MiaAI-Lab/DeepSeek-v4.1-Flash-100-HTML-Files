TITLE: Weather Service — The Sky, Printed Daily

PROMPT:
Create "Weather Service," a self-contained generative weather poster styled as a public utility bulletin. Use cool sky blue, paper cream, sun ochre, rain blue-grey and storm slate. Compose a three-column layout: a serif "Weather service" headline and poetic forecast note; a printed-poster panel with a CSS landscape scene, sun, clouds, rain, layered hills and a tiny city skyline; and a right-hand condition selector plus four-day forecast. Six conditions (Soft, Bright, Rain, Storm, Fog, First Light) each change the sky gradients, sun, cloud opacity, rain overlay, temperature, humidity, wind and newspaper-like copy. Add fine registration borders, bulletin metadata and a paper grain finish. No external images, fonts, libraries or network requests. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A weather page as a piece of everyday print culture. Its illustrations are flat, optimistic and slightly nostalgic, and each condition is treated as a small editorial mood rather than a data dump.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Pure-CSS illustrated weather scene with cloud shapes and layered horizon
- Generative palette and weather-state switching via CSS variables
- Printed-poster framing with registration lines, tilt and grain
- Monospaced meteorological microdata and four-day forecast
- Smooth crossfades and responsive control layout

INTERACTION MODEL:
Select Soft, Bright, Rain, Storm, Fog or First Light. The complete bulletin changes: sky, sun position, cloud density, visible rain, temperature, feels-like, humidity, high/low, wind, forecast values and footer sentence.
