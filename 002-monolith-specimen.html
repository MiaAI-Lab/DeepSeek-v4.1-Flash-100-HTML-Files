R Apex Bowl Junction Leg

The letter as architecture

A grotesque is not a neutral object. Every curve here is loaded: the bowl presses against the stem, the leg kicks out just enough to hold the baseline, and the counter stays open so the form can breathe at 16vw.

Set narrow, it behaves like a headline in a newspaper printed in a hurry. Set wide, it becomes a building.
