TITLE: Chroma Field — A Generative Gradient Study

PROMPT:
Create "Chroma Field," a self-contained generative gradient-art poster page in the spirit of a contemporary gallery. Use warm bone paper, ink black and soft blurred color fields. Compose a three-column layout: an editorial serif "Chroma field" headline; a large portrait poster with a mesh of blurred radial gradient blobs, grain, a thin inner frame and white poster typography; and a right-hand palette panel with swatches, palette name, blob count, and controls. Provide six curated palettes (Terracotta, Coastal, Orchid, Moss & Ember, Ultraviolet, Milk & Rose) and a New Field action that reseeds the composition. The artwork should be clickable to reseed, and the format can toggle between portrait and wide. Add a grain toggle. Everything inline, no external assets, fonts, libraries or network requests. Responsive, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A study in softness and saturated light. The gradient fields behave like weather systems, and the poster framing gives the ephemeral color an object-like presence.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Overlapping radial-gradient blobs with heavy blur and multiply blending
- Grain overlay and inner poster frame
- Generative reseeding with animated movement
- Palette swatches and poster metadata
- Portrait/wide aspect-ratio transition and responsive layout

INTERACTION MODEL:
Click "New field" or the poster itself to generate a new composition. Use "Wide format" to switch the poster between portrait and wide. Toggle grain on/off. Hovering the field causes the gradients to drift slightly.
