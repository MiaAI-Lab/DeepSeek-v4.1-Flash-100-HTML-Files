TITLE: The Loom Room — A Digital Textile Study

PROMPT:
Create "The Loom Room," a self-contained digital weaving atelier that lets a visitor make a small textile. Use warm linen, undyed cotton, madder red, indigo, ochre, sage and dusty rose. Compose an editorial three-column page with a poetic serif title "The loom room," a large wooden-framed 16 × 16 weaving loom in the centre, and a thread cabinet on the right. Show a woven grid of cells over a subtle warp-thread texture, a fringe of swaying tassels, and labels identifying the cloth. Visitors choose a thread colour and click or drag across cells to weave; the counter updates live. Provide three preset patterns (Tide, Meadow, Kilim) and an "Unravel the cloth" reset. All visuals and interactions must be inline, using pure CSS and JavaScript; no external assets, fonts or libraries. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A quiet craft studio in the browser. The feel is of wool, wood and afternoon light; the interaction is slow, tactile and forgiving, inviting the visitor to make a small cloth without instructions or pressure.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- CSS-drawn warp texture and interlaced woven-cell gradients
- Wooden loom frame with fringe tassels and subtle movement
- Drag-painting across a 16×16 grid
- Warm textile palette with editorial serif and monospaced labels
- Pattern presets and live crossing counter

INTERACTION MODEL:
Select a thread from the cabinet, then click or drag across the loom to fill crossings with color. Choose Tide, Meadow or Kilim for an instant pattern, or unravel the cloth to start again. The woven count and a gentle toast respond to every thread placed.
