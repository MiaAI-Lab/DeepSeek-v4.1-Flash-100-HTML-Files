TITLE: Meridian — A Clock for the Long Now

PROMPT:
Create "Meridian," a self-contained luxury astronomical clock page that runs from the visitor's real local time. Use a midnight-black observatory palette with warm ivory, antique gold and a single rust-red second hand. Compose a three-column layout with an elegant high-contrast serif "Meridian hour" headline, a central SVG clock dial with sixty ticks, Roman numerals, concentric arcs, three hands and a rotating gold orbit marker, and a right-hand column of world-city clocks. Put a digital time inside the dial, a live date, zone information, UTC reference, calibration notes and a 12/24-hour control. Selecting London, New York, Tokyo or Marrakesh changes the displayed timezone. All visuals and logic inline; no external assets, libraries or fonts. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
An instrument for measuring the day, designed like something found in a quiet observatory. Fine gold geometry and ivory type give the clock a ceremonial weight, while the rust second hand provides the only constant movement.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Generative SVG clock face with tick marks, Roman numerals and concentric arcs
- Smooth requestAnimationFrame-driven hands in real local time
- Multi-timezone world clock logic using Intl.DateTimeFormat
- High-contrast serif display with technical monospaced annotations
- Grain texture, gold gradients and a restrained dark watch-case composition

INTERACTION MODEL:
The clock runs continuously in the selected city's timezone, with smooth hour, minute and second hands. Choose a city from the right column to recalibrate the entire dial and readout. Click the format control to switch between 24-hour and 12-hour display.
