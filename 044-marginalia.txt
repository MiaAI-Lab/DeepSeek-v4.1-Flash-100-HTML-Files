TITLE: Marginalia — Notes in the Margin

PROMPT:
Create "Marginalia," a self-contained literary essay page designed as an annotated reading copy. Use warm book paper, dark ink, faded olive-grey, pencil blue and a single red pencil accent. Compose a two-column layout: a justified two-column serif essay on paying attention, with a drop cap and five dotted-underline phrases; and a right-hand margin column of notes written in monospaced and handwriting-style type. Each highlighted phrase can be clicked to highlight it and show its corresponding note; notes start visible. Include a "Reading mode" toggle that hides the margin and reflows the essay into a single centred column. Add a red circular reader's stamp, byline, page number and a paper grain texture. No external assets, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A page from a much-loved book, complete with another reader's thoughts. The essay is quiet and generous, and the annotations make the act of reading feel like a conversation across time.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Book-page composition with justified serif prose and drop cap
- Two-column text with margin notes and dotted underline marks
- Handwriting-style annotation accents and pencil texture
- Reading-mode reflow into a single column
- Paper grain, gutter rule and pressed red stamp

INTERACTION MODEL:
Click any underlined phrase to toggle it and its margin note. Press "Reading mode" to hide the notes and centre the essay; press again to restore the annotated copy. Hovering marked phrases gives a soft highlighter wash.
