TITLE: The Correspondence — Three Letters, One Letter Knife

PROMPT:
Create "The Correspondence," a self-contained bundle of three letters on a writing desk. Use dark olive desk, cream laid paper, iron-gall ink, wax red and a muted post-office green. Compose a three-column desk sheet: an editorial "The correspondence" title and three letter buttons (A. Voss to B. Marchetti, B. Marchetti to A. Voss, The Harbour Office to A. Voss); a sealed envelope with a wax seal and folded flap that opens to reveal a written letter sliding upward; and an envelope panel with sender, recipient, paper, ink, seal, pages, a note, Open and Fold away buttons and a receiving stamp. Selecting a letter should reset it to closed and load its content; clicking the envelope or Open should animate the flap, break the seal and raise the letter; Fold away should close it. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A small archive of paper and manners. The interaction is deliberately physical — a seal, a flap, a letter rising — and the writing is period-quiet rather than dramatic.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Envelope with layered flaps and a radial wax seal
- 3D flap rotation and letter-rise transition
- Rule-bordered letter paper with small caps and signature
- Letter data model driving all envelope metadata
- Desk, paper and wax palette with post-office stamp

INTERACTION MODEL:
Choose one of the three letters, then click the envelope or "Open the letter" to break the seal and raise the paper. "Fold it away" closes it. Switching letters always returns the envelope to its sealed state.
