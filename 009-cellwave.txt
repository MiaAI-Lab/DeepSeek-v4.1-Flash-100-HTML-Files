TITLE: Cellwave — Living Culture No. 09

PROMPT:
Create "Cellwave," a self-contained living-systems simulation presented like a contemporary bio-art laboratory. Use a warm peach-paper universe, deep wine typography, coral, lime and a pale milk background. Build an edge-to-edge Canvas petri field containing dozens of translucent, wobbling cells with nuclei and soft membranes; they drift, divide and react to a nutrient flow slider. Overlay an elegant asymmetric editorial interface: huge Georgia "Cell wave" headline, live cell count, culture temperature, generation readout, a barely-there petri dish boundary, and a control bar with Coral / Lime / Indigo dye states. Clicking the culture introduces a new cell. The page should balance scientific precision with sensuous, organic colour. Everything inline; no libraries, images, fonts or network calls. Responsive and valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A bio-art observation deck that makes cell biology feel luminous and tactile. Soft membranes multiply across a peach field while wine-red editorial type, clinical readouts and dye controls create tension between laboratory order and uncontrollable life.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Agent-based Canvas cell simulation with wobbling organic membranes and division
- Layered radial gradients and multiply blending on warm paper
- Offset editorial layout with oversized serif type and technical micro-labels
- Large circular petri-dish framing and grain overlay
- Live scientific counters and palette controls

INTERACTION MODEL:
Move the pointer to stir the culture and bend the cells toward it. Click anywhere to introduce a new organism and update the live count. Choose Coral, Lime or Indigo to dye the colony instantly; drag nutrient flow to accelerate division, movement and the observed generation.
