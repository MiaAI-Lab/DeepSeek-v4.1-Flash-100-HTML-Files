TITLE: The Nickel — A Jukebox with Ten Songs

PROMPT:
Create "The Nickel," a self-contained diner jukebox in cherry red and chrome. Compose a three-column diner sheet: an editorial "The nickel" title and how-it-works note; a Wurlitzer-style cabinet with an arched top, italic neon sign, two columns of animated bubble tubes, a speaker grille, a cream song-card panel listing ten selections (A1–B5) with titles and artists, a volume knob, and a now-playing ticket with a progress bar; and a side panel with the queue, a volume slider, Play, Skip and Clear queue. Clicking a song code should add it to the queue and start it if nothing is playing; tracks should advance automatically when their duration ends; Play/Pause, Skip and Clear should control playback. No external audio, assets, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A jukebox designed as furniture: warm bulbs, chrome, worn paper cards and a queue that behaves like a queue. The music is imaginary, but the machine's manners are exact.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Arched cabinet with layered chrome trim and inset border
- Animated bubble tubes and illuminated speaker grille
- Cream song-card panel with compact two-column typography
- Queue model with automatic track advance and progress bar
- Cherry, chrome and amber palette with neon glow

INTERACTION MODEL:
Click any song card to queue it; the first selection plays immediately. Use Play/Pause, Skip and Clear queue, adjust the volume with the slider or knob, and watch the queue and progress update as tracks finish.
