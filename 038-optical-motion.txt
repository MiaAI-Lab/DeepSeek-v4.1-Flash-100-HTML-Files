TITLE
Optical Study 08 — Motion Is a Decision

PROMPT
Create a bold op-art interactive poster called "Optical Study 08". A white field of concentric black rings sits under a second layer of fine black stripes set at an angle with difference blending, producing moiré interference. A huge black-and-white typographic block in the lower left reads "MOTION IS A DECISION." in tight grotesque capitals, with a single red index label. Moving the pointer warps and rotates the pattern; a frequency slider changes the ring spacing, an invert button flips the field, and a freeze button stops the slow rotation. The composition should feel like a 1960s kinetic poster redesigned for a screen. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS, no external images or fonts.

DESIGN INTENT
Optical, not decorative. The page is an experiment in interference: two simple patterns become complex only through their relationship. Typography is treated as another hard-edged shape, pinned to the lower left with no softness. Red is used exactly once, as a printer's mark would be.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
- Two full-viewport pattern layers: repeating-radial-gradient rings and an angled repeating-linear-gradient of stripes.
- mix-blend-mode:difference for true moiré interference and edge shimmer.
- Pointer-driven translation and rotation of both layers, eased with requestAnimationFrame.
- Frequency slider controlling background-size; invert filter; freeze toggle for rotation.
- Huge tight grotesque display type with a black plate and a small red index.
- Grid marks, coordinates and monospaced captions at the edges.
- Reduced-motion disables the slow rotation and pointer warp, leaving a still optical field.

INTERACTION MODEL
Moving the pointer shifts and rotates the two pattern layers against each other, creating live moiré. The frequency slider changes ring density and stripe scale. "Invert" flips the entire field; "Freeze" pauses the ambient rotation. A click anywhere adds a brief extra rotation jolt. All controls are real buttons and a range input with visible focus; reduced-motion starts frozen.
