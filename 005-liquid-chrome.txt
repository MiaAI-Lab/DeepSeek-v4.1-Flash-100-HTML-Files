TITLE: Liquid Chrome — Soft Metal Study

PROMPT:
Create "Liquid Chrome," a self-contained experimental material-study page about soft metal. The canvas is a near-black room containing an organic, slowly morphing pool of chrome made from layered radial gradients, highlights and thin orbital contour rings. Overlay enormous typography reading "Soft metal" — uppercase sans for "Soft" and an italic serif lowercase for "metal" — with a difference blend mode, plus scientific micro-labels, a live surface temperature, and a small tilted "Drag to form" token. The palette is black, warm white, graphite and a sharp citron highlight. Make the chrome respond fluidly to pointer position and press. Add three material presets (Chrome, Mercury, Black oil) that recolor the metal. The result should feel like a digital object in a high-end industrial design catalogue. All CSS, JS and visual matter inline; no external dependencies. Responsive and valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
An austere industrial design study where liquid chrome appears to float in a black studio. Monumental type is cut by the reflective material, while microscopic technical annotations and a single citron accent provide the tension of a luxury product catalogue.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas 2D metaball-like chrome rendered from overlapping radial gradients
- Difference-blend typography interacting with the metal
- Animated specular sheen and fine elliptical contour rings
- Inline SVG grain and vignette overlays
- Technical microcopy, monospaced readouts and minimal pill controls

INTERACTION MODEL:
Move the pointer across the canvas to guide the chrome pool; press and drag to compress it and trigger a fresh surface-temperature reading. Choose Chrome, Mercury or Black Oil to transform the material palette. Touch input is supported, and the material drifts on its own when left still.
