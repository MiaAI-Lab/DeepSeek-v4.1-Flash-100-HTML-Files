TITLE: Motif Mill — A Textile Pattern Studio

PROMPT:
Create "Motif Mill," a self-contained generative textile-pattern studio styled like a small block-printing atelier. Use warm linen, undyed cotton, madder red, indigo, ochre, sage and ink. Compose a three-column layout: a serif "Motif mill" headline and short note; a large square cloth sample with a Canvas-drawn repeating grid pattern, inner plate border, slight tilt and printed sample label; and a right-hand control column with repeat slider, five palette swatches, six motif buttons and a New Sample action. The Canvas should draw a tiled pattern of blooms, diamonds, stripes, arcs, crosses or dots with weave texture, cell variants and subtle grid lines. Clicking the cloth should alter the corresponding row and column. Everything inline; no external assets, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A digital textile studio where patterns are treated as small systems. The cloth feels printed rather than rendered, and every generated sample has the specificity of a real bolt of fabric.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas 2D tiled motif generation with per-cell variants
- Faux weave texture from fine overlaid lines
- Palette and motif state driving redraw
- Cloth sample presentation with plate border and label
- Row/column response to clicking the pattern

INTERACTION MODEL:
Adjust repeat from 3 to 12. Choose Madder, Indigo, Ochre, Sage or Ink. Choose Bloom, Diamond, Stripe, Arc, Cross or Dot to redraw the repeat. Click any cell to mutate its row and column; press New Sample for a new configuration and sample number.
