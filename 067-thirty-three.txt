TITLE: Thirty-Three & a Third — A Record and a Sleeve

PROMPT:
Create "Thirty-Three & a Third," a self-contained record-playing page for a fictional album. Use warm black, cream, coral red and cobalt blue. Compose a three-column listening room: a square album sleeve with a bold geometric inline-SVG design (cobalt block, coral sun, black field, cream disc, title "Low Tide"); a CSS turntable with platter, grooved record, coral centre label, steel tonearm, 33/45 speed buttons and a strobe; and a tracklist with five songs, timings, release data, a now-playing readout, Play/Pause and Next track. Pressing Play should spin the record, swing the tonearm onto it, light the strobe, animate a level meter and run a track timer; the record should stop when paused. Speed buttons should change the rotation speed and label. No external assets, audio files, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A record sleeve and a turntable, designed as objects rather than as a music player UI. The interaction is deliberately physical: one switch, one arm, one record that begins to turn.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Repeating-radial-gradient vinyl grooves and specular sheen
- CSS tonearm with pivot, arm and headshell, rotating into playing position
- Spinning platter animation with speed control
- Inline-SVG geometric sleeve artwork
- Animated level meter and live track timer

INTERACTION MODEL:
Press Play to start the record, tonearm, strobe and timer; press Pause to stop them. Click any track to cue it, press Next track to advance, and choose 33 or 45 to change the rotation speed and the label text.
