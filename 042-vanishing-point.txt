TITLE: Vanishing Point — A Study in Reflection

PROMPT:
Create "Vanishing Point," a self-contained generative mirror-room artwork. Use near-black depth, luminous mint, soft rose and muted ivory. The centrepiece is a full-screen Canvas recursion of nested octagonal frames drawn one within another, each slightly rotated, shrinking and fading toward a glowing vanishing point. Pointer movement should lean the entire recursive room, and clicking should send out an elliptical ripple of light. Overlay a restrained editorial interface: a serif "Vanishing point" headline with an outlined italic second line, a recursion slider, visible-room counter and three light modes (Cold, Warm, Noir). All visuals and motion inline; no external images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A hypnotic architectural daydream built from repetition. Each reflection is a slightly different room, and the glow at the centre feels less like a destination than a quiet disappearance.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Recursive Canvas 2D octagonal frame composition
- Pointer-driven skew and rotation for physical mirror-room feel
- Additive radial glow and click ripples
- Outlined display typography with gradient accents
- Three color-temperature modes and live recursion control

INTERACTION MODEL:
Move the pointer to tilt the room. Click anywhere to release an elliptical ripple and change the footer status. Use the Recursion slider to deepen or simplify the mirror sequence and switch between Cold, Warm and Noir lighting.
