TITLE: Prism — A Study of Dispersion

PROMPT:
Create "Prism," a self-contained interactive optical study of white light splitting into a spectrum. Use a near-black darkroom, warm ivory type, cool glassy prism fills and the full visible spectrum. Build a Canvas 2D scene with a single white beam entering a triangular glass prism and fanning into seven colored rays. Add orbiting technical rings, tick marks and scientific labels. Overlay an editorial layout: Georgia headline "White light" where "light" is gradient-filled, a short meditation, and a right-hand instrument readout with incident angle, refractive index, medium name, deviation and a prism rotation slider. Moving the pointer steers the incoming beam. Rotating the slider changes the prism angle and updates the optical readings. No external assets, fonts, images, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A darkroom instrument where physics becomes theatre. The prism is a piece of cold glass in a warm black field, and the spectrum is handled with the care of a patient scientific illustration.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas 2D light transport with additive blending and glow
- Gradient-filled typography using background-clip text
- Rotating prism geometry and technical instrument rings
- Pointer-driven beam entry angle
- Live optical readouts and slider-driven state

INTERACTION MODEL:
Move the pointer to aim the incoming white beam. Drag the prism rotation slider to tilt the glass, change refractive index and medium classification, and update incident angle and deviation readouts in real time. The whole scene responds smoothly through interpolation.
