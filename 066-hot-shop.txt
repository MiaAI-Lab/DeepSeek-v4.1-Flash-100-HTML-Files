TITLE: The Hot Shop — Five Stages of Glass

PROMPT:
Create "The Hot Shop," a self-contained glassblowing studio page in five stages. Use soot black, iron grey, warm cream, ember orange and molten gold. Compose a three-column bench: an editorial "Hot glass" title with five clickable stages (Gather, Marver, Blow, Shape, Anneal) and their temperatures; a furnace scene with a steel blowpipe and a glowing glass gather built from radial gradients, a subtle sway animation and rising sparks; and a control panel with stage name, tool, description, glass temperature, colour, rotation, stage count, a heat slider and next-step status. Selecting a stage should set the temperature, change the gather's border-radius and scale, and update all readouts. Dragging the heat slider should change the glow, colour name and closest stage live. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
The drama of a hot shop condensed to one glowing form. The piece of glass is a single CSS shape whose colour, glow, proportion and outline shift with heat and stage, so the page feels warm without a single image.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Multi-stop radial gradients simulating molten glass
- Layered box-shadow glow that scales with temperature
- CSS shape morphing through border-radius and transform
- Animated sway and rising ember particles
- Temperature slider mapped to colour names and stage selection

INTERACTION MODEL:
Click Gather, Marver, Blow, Shape or Anneal to set the stage, temperature, vessel shape and all readouts. Drag the heat slider to cool or heat the glass continuously; the glow, colour name, state description and nearest stage update as you move.
