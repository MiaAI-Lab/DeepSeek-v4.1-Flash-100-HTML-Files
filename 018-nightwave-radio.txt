TITLE: Nightwave FM — Radio for the Awake

PROMPT:
Create "Nightwave FM," a self-contained risograph-inspired radio station site for insomniacs. Use warm cream paper, deep petrol blue, fluorescent pink and a small yellow accent. Build a two-colour print aesthetic with visible halftone dots, overprint texture and slightly off-register typography. Compose a bold poster layout: huge black "Radio for the awake" headline, a blurb, and a detailed illustrated radio receiver with a circular speaker grille, glowing frequency readout, station name, rotating tuning knob, tuning arrows and animated equalizer bars. Include station schedule, ON AIR indicator, transmission notes and print-spec colophon. Tuning changes frequency, station, now-playing text and knob angle. No external assets, fonts, images or libraries. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A late-night independent radio station as a beloved physical object. Riso pink and petrol ink overlap on warm paper, the hand-drawn receiver feels tactile, and the typography has the confident imperfection of a screen-printed gig poster.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Two-colour risograph palette with halftone and grain overlays
- Illustrated CSS radio with speaker grille, dial and physical knob
- Draggable/rotatable tuning control using CSS custom properties
- Animated equalizer bars and blinking ON AIR signal
- Poster-scale grotesk headings with monospaced programme listings

INTERACTION MODEL:
Use the arrow buttons or drag the radio knob vertically to tune between five late-night stations. Frequency, station name, now-playing line and knob position update together. The knob also responds to left/right arrow keys when focused.
