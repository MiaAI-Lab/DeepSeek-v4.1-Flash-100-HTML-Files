TITLE
Nocturne — We Are Made of Old Light

PROMPT
Create a full-viewport generative star-field experience called "Nocturne". On load, thousands of tiny stars form the word "NOCTURNE" across a deep midnight sky, then drift slowly; a few nearby stars are joined by faint cyan constellation lines. Moving the pointer repels the nearest stars and clicking them sends a ripple through the field before it reforms. The composition is cinematic and minimal: an index at the top, a large editorial statement "We are made of old light." in the lower left, and a small technical readout in the lower right. Palette: near-black navy (#04060d), starlight ivory (#e4ebff), a single warm amber star and pale cyan lines. Use a monospaced scientific vocabulary, a serif italic accent and very restrained UI. The canvas must be the hero. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS and JS, no external libraries, images or fonts.

DESIGN INTENT
A quiet, astronomical version of a hero section — closer to an observatory plate than a website header. The word is legible as a constellation only when you look for it; individual stars remain distinct points with their own brightness and drift. UI is placed at the edges like annotations on a star chart, leaving the centre to the sky. The single amber star is the emotional anchor.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
- Offscreen canvas text rasterization to sample the word NOCTURNE into star positions.
- Particle system with depth, brightness, easing toward target positions and subtle twinkle.
- Proximity repulsion around the pointer; click impulse and spring return.
- Constellation lines drawn between stars within a distance threshold, with a cap for performance.
- Radial nebula gradients, scattered dim stars and a faint horizon glow.
- Retina-aware canvas sizing, throttled resize and a reduced-motion static star chart.
- Edge-anchored editorial typography and monospaced technical readouts.

INTERACTION MODEL
Stars drift and twinkle continuously. Moving the pointer pushes nearby stars away; releasing lets them ease back. Clicking anywhere emits a ripple and briefly scatters the constellation. A "trace" button toggles constellation lines, and a "scatter / reform" control animates the whole field. Keyboard control: the trace and scatter actions are buttons, and reduced-motion renders a still, fully legible star word.
