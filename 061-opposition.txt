TITLE: The Opposition — A King and Pawn Study

PROMPT:
Create "The Opposition," a self-contained interactive king-and-pawn chess study. Use deep green felt, warm wooden board squares, ivory and charcoal pieces, and brass highlights. Compose a three-column layout: an editorial title and explanation of the opposition; a working 8×8 chess board with algebraic coordinates and Unicode chess pieces; and a side panel with whose turn it is, a status note, a move log, reset and key-square controls. Start from White Ke5, pawn e4, Black Ke7, White to move. Allow the player to select the white king or pawn, show legal destination dots and capture rings, make legal moves, and have the black king reply automatically with a legal move. Show the pawn's key promotion square in brass. Detect promotion, capture, stalemate and check-like adjacency. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A chess lesson you can touch. The board is the argument and the side panel is the teacher, keeping the tone analytical while the brass key square quietly shows what all the manoeuvring is for.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- CSS grid chessboard with light/dark squares and coordinate labels
- Unicode piece rendering with legal-move overlays
- Move generation for kings and pawns, including capture and promotion
- Automatic opposing-king reply and move log
- Brass key-square overlay toggle and status narration

INTERACTION MODEL:
Click the white king or pawn to see legal squares, then click a highlighted square to move. Black replies automatically. Use Reset study to restore the position and Toggle key squares to show or hide the promotion target. The status panel narrates the position.
