TITLE: Book of Hours 075 — Eight Prayers for the Day

PROMPT:
Create "Book of Hours 075," a self-contained illuminated manuscript page for eight canonical hours. Use vellum cream, iron-gall black, gold leaf, lapis blue and vermilion. Compose a three-column codex leaf: a serif "Little hours" title with eight hour buttons from Matins to Compline; a ruled manuscript page with a large gilded decorated initial, two columns of Latin prayer text with red rubrics and a gold doxology, two small illuminated miniatures and a marginal vine; and a side panel with hour name, time, note and material specifications. Selecting an hour should change the initial, prayer, rubrics, hour label and note. Keep the manuscript texture, borders and script-like typography consistent. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A devotional page that behaves like a book. The illumination is restrained, the Latin is set to be read slowly, and the eight hours give the reader a reason to turn the leaf.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Gilded initial with layered inset borders
- Two-column manuscript text with red rubrics and gold words
- Inline-SVG miniatures for sky and office
- Marginal vine decoration and vellum texture
- Responsive reduction that protects the page at small sizes

INTERACTION MODEL:
Choose Matins, Lauds, Prime, Terce, Sext, None, Vespers or Compline. The page crossfades to that hour's initial, prayer, time and note, and the active hour is marked in the index.
