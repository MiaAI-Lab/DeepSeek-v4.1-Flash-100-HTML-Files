TITLE
MUSA — A Mosaic Atelier

PROMPT
Create a tactile mosaic studio called "MUSA". Warm stone background, a framed mosaic panel of hundreds of tiny tessera tiles, and a narrow atelier control rail with six stone colours (terracotta, ochre, sea, ivory, black, olive), three pattern buttons (Medallion, Wave, Scatter), a Clear control and a placed-tile counter. The mosaic is a 24×16 grid with visible grout, slightly irregular tiles and a classical rosette generated on load. Visitors paint by clicking or dragging across the panel; pattern buttons regenerate a symmetrical medallion or wave composition; Clear empties the grout. Include a museum plaque, a gold double frame and a footer. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS, no external images or fonts.

DESIGN INTENT
The physical pleasure of small tiles. The panel should feel heavy and old: deep grout, stone colours, a double gold frame, a plaque screwed to the bottom. The controls are workshop tools, not app widgets. The generated medallion gives an immediate "how was that made?" moment before the visitor starts painting.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
- CSS grid mosaic with grout lines, per-tile subtle rotation and tonal variation.
- Deterministic medallion generator using ring and angle functions from the tile's coordinates.
- Wave and scatter generators, plus free painting by pointer drag.
- Six stone colours with slight per-tile brightness variation.
- Double gold frame, museum plaque and a soft cast shadow.
- Atelier control rail with colour swatches, pattern tools and a tile counter.
- Responsive mosaic scaling; reduced-motion removes tile transitions.

INTERACTION MODEL
Selecting a stone colour and painting across the grid places tiles instantly. Medallion, Wave and Scatter regenerate the whole composition; Clear removes every tile. The count updates live. Pattern buttons have pressed states, colour swatches are accessible buttons, and reduced-motion keeps all states without transitions.
