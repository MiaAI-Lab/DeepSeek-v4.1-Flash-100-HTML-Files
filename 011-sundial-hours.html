Note 01 — the equation

Noon is never on time.

The gap between clock noon and solar noon shifts through the year by up to sixteen minutes, a wobble caused by an elliptical orbit and a tilted axis.

±16′
Note 02 — the shadow

Light leaves a ruler.

At this latitude the noon shadow doubles in winter and almost disappears in June. The dial reads the season as much as the hour.

1 : 3.2
Note 03 — the slower hour

Every sundial runs slow.

Not a fault — a refusal to round. A dial divides the day by light, not by agreement, and so keeps a little of the afternoon for itself.
