The

A mapmaker tries to draw the room he is falling asleep in. The room, politely, declines to hold still.

Written & drawn in one file
Paper ivory · ink blue · 6 pages
Chapter onep. 1

The room, at first.

The cartographer began with the window, because windows are easy: a rectangle, a cross, a little weather beyond. Then the desk refused its outline. Then the chair.

1
Chapter onep. 2

The desk, unruly.

He drew the desk four times and each version was more like a boat. He decided to keep the fourth. Maps are allowed to be honest about the wrong thing.

2
Chapter twop. 3

Outside, a sea.

Beyond the window, the street had become a very small sea. A boat passed. He drew it in the margin, where impossible things are allowed to dock.

3
Chapter twop. 4

The map, growing.

By midnight the room contained a harbour, two mountains and a small republic of sleep. He labelled nothing. Naming is a kind of closing.

4
Chapter threep. 5

He slept, briefly.

The pencil rolled to the floor. The room, relieved, returned to being a room. In the morning the map was still there, and slightly more accurate than before.
