TITLE
Haptic — A Softer Control Surface

PROMPT
Design a neumorphic "calm technology" control surface called Haptic, presented as a single sculpted device floating on a pale, dotted studio backdrop. The palette is cool porcelain (#e8ebf2) with warm coral, mint, sky and lilac accents; every control is extruded from the same material with paired light and dark shadows, generous radii and no hard borders. The device contains a large rotatable dial with a glowing inset ring, three tactile toggle switches, four mood presets (Calm, Focus, Warm, Night), and a soft oscilloscope canvas whose wave amplitude follows the dial. Include a small brand header, a right-hand live readout ("state / intensity / tone"), and quiet microcopy. The mood should be closer to a Dieter Rams appliance than a toy: restrained, tactile, premium. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file, inline CSS and JS, no external assets.

DESIGN INTENT
An antidote to neon: a soft, physical interface with the visual weight of a well-made object. Neumorphism is used with discipline — one material, four accents, deep but diffuse shadows — so the page reads as industrial design rather than a CSS effect. The dial is the hero and the oscilloscope gives the object a pulse; everything else stays quiet around them.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
- Neumorphic extrusion using paired box-shadows (light top-left, dark bottom-right) and inset variants for pressed states.
- Porcelain surface palette with four accent tokens that recolour the dial halo, canvas stroke and status lights.
- Large circular dial built from conic-gradient tick marks, a rotating indicator and an inset glass ring.
- Dotted studio backdrop with a soft radial vignette and a floating floor shadow beneath the device.
- Canvas oscilloscope with smoothed sine composition, adapted for devicePixelRatio.
- Tactile toggle switches with extruded tracks and inset thumbs; pill preset buttons with pressed states.
- Fluid responsive layout that stacks the dial above the controls on narrow screens.

INTERACTION MODEL
The dial can be dragged (pointer) or adjusted with arrow keys to change intensity from 0–100; its ring rotates and the oscilloscope amplitude follows. Four mood presets recolour the accent and update the readout, toggle states and dial position with a soft spring transition, accompanied by an optional quiet WebAudio chime (created only after a user gesture). Toggles press in and out with inset shadows. All controls have focus rings and ARIA roles; reduced-motion removes springs and canvas animation.
