TITLE: The Long Scale — A Solar System at Human Pace

PROMPT:
Create "The Long Scale," a self-contained scrollable journey through the solar system, designed as an editorial astronomy essay rather than a science app. Use near-black space, warm ivory type, and a single solar amber accent. Begin with a full-screen hero: serif "The long scale" title, a note about scale (1:1.4 billion), and a scroll cue. Then present the Sun and eight planets as alternating editorial sections, each with a number, name, Latin subtitle, short poetic text, three stats, and a CSS-rendered planet with appropriate color, bands, ring system or moon, plus distance label. Add a fixed HUD with a progress line and live AU distance. End with a quiet coda about the Kuiper Belt and return link. No external images, fonts, libraries or network requests. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A long walk through emptiness, made beautiful. Scale is conveyed through scroll length and generous negative space, while each planet is a carefully shaded CSS sphere with just enough detail to feel observed.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- CSS planet spheres with layered radial gradients, bands and inset shading
- Saturn ring system and Earth's moon in pure CSS
- Long-form scrolling layout with alternating sections
- Fixed HUD with live progress and AU readout
- Editorial serif typography over a star-field background

INTERACTION MODEL:
Scroll to travel outward from the Sun to Neptune. The HUD progress line and distance readout update continuously, and the current nearest body name changes. The end section offers a smooth return to the Sun.
