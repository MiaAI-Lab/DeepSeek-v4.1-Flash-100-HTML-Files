TITLE: Velvet House — A Cinema for the Attentive

PROMPT:
Create "Velvet House," a self-contained luxury independent-cinema site with an editorial, mid-century European mood. Use warm cream, deep wine, antique gold, midnight blue and dusty rose. Compose a two-column cinematic layout: an elegant high-contrast serif title "Velvet House" and film metadata on the left, and a stage-like screen with deep-red velvet curtains on the right. Behind the curtains, build an abstract film still entirely in CSS and inline SVG: a glowing horizon, dark landscape and a solitary figure. Add a "Open the curtains" button with a smooth theater reveal, a film programme of three screenings, seat counts and a toast transmission. Selecting a film changes the poster gradients, title, runtime and seat availability. Everything must stay self-contained with no external assets, fonts, images or libraries. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
An independent picture house distilled into a poster-like digital object. Rich wine curtains, an abstract glow and restrained gold typography create anticipation before the film has even begun.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Theatrical curtain reveal using layered gradients and 3D-feeling transform transitions
- Pure-CSS abstract film still with horizon, silhouette and atmospheric light
- Luxury editorial composition with high-contrast serif and monospaced programme data
- Film selector that recolors the scene and updates metadata
- Paper grain overlay and understated toast feedback

INTERACTION MODEL:
Press "Open the curtains" to part the velvet panels and reveal the screen; press again to close them. Choose one of three screenings to change the film title, runtime, poster palette and remaining seats. Booking-style feedback appears as a quiet toast at the bottom.
