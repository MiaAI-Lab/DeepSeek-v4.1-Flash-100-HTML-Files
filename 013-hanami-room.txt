TITLE: Hanami Room — A Quiet Place for Falling Things

PROMPT:
Create "Hanami Room," a self-contained digital tea house for watching cherry blossoms fall. Use a warm rice-paper ground, sumi ink, muted vermillion, dusty blush and moss. Build an airy three-column composition: vertical Japanese display type reading 物の哀れ, a large arched window filled with a soft gradient dusk, a sun disc and hand-drawn inline-SVG blossom branches; and a right-hand haiku with a wind control. A full-screen Canvas petal field drifts in front of and behind the interface. The page should feel calm, literary, tactile and expensive. Include blossom counter, drift direction, seasonal stamps and quiet microcopy. Wind slider changes density and movement; clicking/ tapping releases a small flurry of petals. No external assets, fonts, libraries or network requests. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A meditative seasonal room where Japanese typography, a blush dusk window and drifting cherry petals create stillness. Every detail — from the red hanko stamp to the haiku — is arranged with the restraint of a private tea house.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Full-screen Canvas petals with per-petal gradient, wobble and rotation
- Arched CSS window with layered sun, mountain horizon and inline SVG branches
- Vertical Japanese display typography alongside English serif poetry
- Rice-paper grain, red seal stamp and monospaced seasonal metadata
- Responsive vertical-to-horizontal recomposition

INTERACTION MODEL:
Adjust the wind slider to change petal speed, density and drift language. Move the pointer gently to influence the breeze, and click or tap anywhere to release a fresh handful of blossoms. The release counter increments as petals drift off-screen.
