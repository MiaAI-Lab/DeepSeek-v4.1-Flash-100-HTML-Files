Poem / six movements / after the rain

Small
Weather

A poem for the hour between putting the kettle on and deciding to go outside. Written in the margins of a weather report.

— for whoever is reading this
01
The weather begins indoors.
Movement I — Indoors1 / 6
02
A kettle exhales. The window decides to become a horizon.
Movement II — Kettle2 / 6
03
We are all small maps of places we have not left yet.
Movement III — Maps3 / 6
04
Rain writes in the margin. We read it as permission.
Movement IV — Margin4 / 6
05
Somewhere a door is a question left open.
Movement V — Door5 / 6
06
Take the long way home. The long way is the weather.
Movement VI — Home6 / 6
and the kettle, again,

begin small.
