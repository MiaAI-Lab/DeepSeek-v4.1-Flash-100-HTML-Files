TITLE: Terra Serial — A Living Index of the Planet

PROMPT:
Build "Terra Serial," a self-contained Swiss-editorial climate field journal that treats planetary data as living poetry. Use a pale mineral green paper field, deep forest ink, clay orange and an electric lime accent. Compose a bold asymmetric layout with a giant condensed uppercase "TERRA" and outlined "SERIAL" anchored by topographic contour lines, a left-aligned manifesto, and a narrow index panel listing observed planetary states with slim data bars. Add a live temperature readout, index status, issue micro-type, and an orbiting annotation. A custom "observe" cursor note and acid highlight follow index hovers; clicking an index row updates the observation readout. Keep the typography crisp and art-directed. No external dependencies; all effects in inline CSS, inline SVG and JavaScript. Responsive, offline-ready, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A climate broadsheet with the confident spatial rhythm of a modern design biennial. Topographic contours draw themselves behind enormous condensed type, while a precise scientific index and live temperature transform ecological data into a calm, collectible visual language.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Swiss-inspired asymmetric grid and dramatic type scale
- Outlined display lettering with a clay punctuation dot
- Self-drawing inline SVG topographic contour lines
- Data bars, monospaced readings and live numeric ticker
- Contextual cursor-following label and lime click highlight

INTERACTION MODEL:
Hover the planetary index to summon a small observation label that follows the pointer. Click any index row to update the status with its name and measurement and flash the row in acid lime. The temperature footer drifts gently every few seconds, making the scientific record feel continuously sampled.
