Genus no. 01 / cumulus

Cumulusthe everyday

Cumulus humilis — fair-weather heap

Specimen drawn at 2,400 m / midday
Fig. 1 — Cumulus humilis, shown from the west— 14 —
Altitude0.5 – 2 km
AppearanceDetached, cotton-like
PrecipitationNone
Latin rootCumulus / a heap

The most familiar cloud, and the least examined. Cumulus forms when warm air rises and cools just enough to condense. It is the sky’s handwriting: soft, provisional, always about to change its mind.
