TITLE
World Foundry — A Planet in Four Sliders

PROMPT
Create a planet-generation laboratory called "World Foundry". Deep space black with a soft nebula glow, a large procedurally drawn planet on the right, and a narrow control column on the left. Four sliders control radius, water, cloud cover and temperature; four presets (Ocean, Desert, Ice, Gas) apply curated combinations; "Forge a new world" randomises and generates a new name and seed. The planet is drawn on canvas with bands, continents, cloud swirls, polar caps, a terminator shadow and an atmosphere rim, plus one small moon on an orbit. Readouts show radius, water, cloud, mean temperature, gravity and a generated designation. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS, no external images or fonts.

DESIGN INTENT
Scientific wonder, carefully typeset. The planet should look plausible at a glance — bands, clouds, ice caps, a dark limb — and beautiful at a second glance. The UI is an observatory instrument: thin rules, tiny labels, numeric readouts and one warm accent for the button. The dark room makes the planet the only source of colour.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
- Canvas planet with layered gradients, latitude bands, seeded continent and cloud blobs, polar caps and a terminator shadow.
- Atmospheric rim glow and a faint outer haze drawn with radial gradients.
- Star field, nebula wash, orbit rings and an animated moon.
- Sliders with live numeric readouts and preset buttons.
- Generated planet designations from syllable pools; stable seed while adjusting sliders.
- Editorial serif title and monospaced scientific metadata.
- Responsive layout with the planet above the controls on mobile; reduced-motion stops moon and twinkle animation.

INTERACTION MODEL
Adjusting any slider redraws the planet immediately and updates the corresponding readout and classification. Preset buttons set all four values and redraw. "Forge a new world" picks a new seed, name and values. The moon orbits continuously; clicking the planet creates a small scan pulse. All controls are keyboard accessible; reduced-motion draws a still scene.
