TITLE: Lanterns 073 — A Wish Released onto the Water

PROMPT:
Create "Lanterns 073," a self-contained night scene of paper lanterns released onto water. Use deep teal night, indigo water, warm amber and gold light, and soft cream type. Build a full-screen atmospheric scene with a gradient sky, moon, twinkling stars, layered hill silhouettes, a water plane with drifting ripple lines and a field of glowing paper lanterns rising and swaying. Overlay an editorial "Release a wish" title, a wish input with a Release button, a lantern counter and a small list of recent wishes. Releasing a wish should spawn a lantern at a random point on the water with the wish written on it, animate it upward for roughly fifteen seconds, increment the counter and add the wish to the recent list; ambient lanterns should also rise periodically. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A quiet participatory scene rather than a poster. The lanterns do all the visual work, and the tiny wish text turns a decorative animation into a small ritual the visitor can take part in.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- CSS lanterns with layered glow, inner frame and hanging tab
- Randomized spawn position, sway, scale and float duration
- Drifting water ripples and twinkling star field
- Wish text attached to each lantern and mirrored in a recent list
- Night palette with amber light discipline

INTERACTION MODEL:
Type a wish and press Release (or Enter). A lantern carries it up from the water, the released count increases and the wish appears in the recent list. Ambient lanterns continue to rise on their own.
