TITLE
Contour No. 6 — Read the Ground

PROMPT
Create a cartographic instrument called "Contour No. 6". A large paper-toned map canvas draws real contour lines from a procedural elevation field, with index contours heavier, a fine survey grid and a warm terracotta accent. Overlay a serif title "Read the ground.", a legend of elevation bands, and a live coordinate and elevation readout. Below, a survey control bar holds three sliders (Relief, Detail, Contours), preset buttons (Island, Ridge, Valley), a re-seed button and a grid toggle. Clicking the map drops a survey marker. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS/canvas, no external images or fonts.

DESIGN INTENT
A map you would find folded in a field notebook: warm paper, hairline contours, index lines in rust, tiny coordinates. The tool should feel analog and precise rather than like a dashboard. Every slider changes the terrain immediately, and the same seed always produces the same landscape.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
- Marching-squares contour generation from a seeded multi-octave elevation field.
- Gaussian hill presets combined with value noise; normalisation to a consistent range.
- Index contours every fourth level, thinner intermediate contours, survey grid and edge ticks.
- Live elevation and coordinate readout on pointer move; click-to-place survey marker.
- Paper texture, letterpress-like labels and terracotta accent.
- Responsive canvas with device-pixel-ratio scaling.
- Reduced-motion removes the short redraw fade.

INTERACTION MODEL
Moving the pointer reads the elevation at that point and draws a crosshair. Clicking places or moves a survey marker with its coordinates. Relief, Detail and Contours sliders redraw the terrain; Island, Ridge and Valley presets change its basic form; Re-seed changes the noise; Grid toggles the survey grid. All controls are labelled and keyboard accessible.
