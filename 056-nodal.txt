TITLE: Nodal — Chladni Figures in Sand

PROMPT:
Create "Nodal," a self-contained acoustic laboratory page that visualises Chladni figures. Use a blackened steel plate, warm sand white and muted brass on a near-black background, with an editorial serif "Chladni figures" title. The centrepiece is a large circular plate drawn on canvas where 2,400 sand grains settle along the nodal lines of a standing wave. Implement the Chladni equation cos(nπx)cos(mπy) − cos(mπx)cos(nπy) and move each grain down the gradient of the absolute field so it gathers where the plate is still, with a small jitter that decays over time. Provide six resonant presets (Cross, Star, Web, Lace, Quilt, Filigree) that set the n and m modes; display mode numbers, estimated frequency, grain count and symmetry. Keep the composition instrument-like, with monospaced readouts and a quiet footer. No external assets, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A physics demonstration treated as a design object. The sand is real particle motion rather than a drawn pattern, so every preset resolves out of noise into an intricate figure that could only have come from the equation.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas particle simulation of the Chladni standing-wave equation
- Gradient-descent settling with decaying jitter
- Radial plate shading and circular masking
- Brass-and-sand instrument palette with engraved micro-typography
- Live mode, frequency, symmetry and grain readouts

INTERACTION MODEL:
Choose any of the six resonant presets to change the n and m modes. The sand is disturbed and then re-settles into the new nodal figure over a couple of seconds, while the readouts update. The plate animates continuously.
