TITLE: Neon Sumi — A Luminous Ink Garden

PROMPT:
Create "Neon Sumi," a self-contained luminous ink-painting studio that blends Japanese sumi-e brushwork with neon light. Use deep indigo night, electric cyan, hot pink, violet and pale ochre. Build a full-screen Canvas 2D painting surface with a dark silk weave texture, a vignette and a pre-drawn set of glowing mountain-ink strokes. The visitor can drag to paint tapered, glowing brush lines; choose ink colors (Cyan, Pink, Violet, Ochre), change brush size, add mountains and clear the paper. Overlay an editorial layout with a large serif "Neon sumi" headline, outlined pink second line, a short verse in the upper right, and a footer with tools and counters. Everything inline; no external assets, fonts, libraries or network requests. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A nocturne rendered in light. The page treats the screen as a sheet of black silk where each gesture glows briefly and then belongs to the painting, balancing contemplative Japanese ink aesthetics with synthetic color.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas 2D tapered glowing brush strokes with shadowBlur
- Pre-drawn neon mountain silhouettes with layered fills
- Silk-weave texture and vignette overlays
- Outlined display typography and gradient rule details
- Responsive painting surface with touch and pen support

INTERACTION MODEL:
Drag or draw anywhere on the canvas to paint with the selected ink. Choose Cyan, Pink, Violet or Ochre; adjust brush thickness with the slider; press Add Mountains to layer more neon peaks; press Clear Paper to wipe the surface and reseed the mountain composition.
