TITLE: The Tea House — Five Teas, One Quiet Pot

PROMPT:
Create "The Tea House," a self-contained tea-brewing page with a working timer. Use warm clay brown, cream, celadon green and tea amber. Compose a three-column tea room: an editorial "The tea house" title and five tea buttons (Gyokuro, Darjeeling, Tieguanyin, Lapsang, Rooibos); a celadon bowl with animated steam and a circular SVG timer ring showing countdown; and a panel with tea name, subtitle, note, type, origin, water temperature, steep time, leaf amount, infusions, a strength bar and tasting notes. Selecting a tea should set its temperature, time, leaf amount and notes. Start should run a real countdown, update the ring and strength bar, pause/resume, and report when the tea is ready; Reset should restore the full steep. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A brewing page that asks you to wait. The steam and the timer ring make the waiting visible, and every tea has its own temperature and patience built into the interaction.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Celadon bowl in CSS with gradient glaze and specular highlight
- Animated wisp of steam rising from the bowl
- Circular SVG countdown ring with live stroke offset
- Tea data model driving temperature, time and leaf amount
- Clay, celadon and amber palette with quiet typography

INTERACTION MODEL:
Choose one of five teas to load its brewing parameters. Press Start steeping to begin the countdown; pause or resume at any time, and Reset to return to the full time. The strength bar and status line update as the tea steeps.
