A sheet for the unpractised hand

One breath,
one line.

The paper is more patient than the painter. Drag anywhere to leave a mark — slow for weight, quick for air. Nothing here can be undone, which is part of the lesson.

A circle not closed
still holds the morning
— after Issa

The sheet is ready.
