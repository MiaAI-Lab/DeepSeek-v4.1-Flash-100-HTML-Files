Three ingredients, no shortcuts

Cacioe pepe

Pecorino, black pepper and the water the pasta cooked in. A recipe that fails if you hurry it, and rewards you if you do not.

Scale the recipe

Rigatoni · pecorino romano · black pepper
Plated at the pass, 22 cm

Method

01Boil the pasta in well-salted water. Keep a mug of the cooking water before you drain it.9 min
02Toast the pepper in a dry pan until it smells warm, then add a little pasta water.1 min
03Mix the pecorino with pasta water in a bowl until it becomes a smooth paste, not a lumpy one.2 min
04Toss the drained pasta with the pepper and a splash of water, then take it off the heat.1 min
05Fold in the cheese paste off the heat. Stir hard. Serve immediately, in warm bowls.30 sec

Cook's noteIf the sauce seizes, add pasta water one spoon at a time and keep stirring. Heat is what breaks it; patience is what brings it back.
