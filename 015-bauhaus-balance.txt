TITLE: Bauhaus Balance — A Study in Visual Weight

PROMPT:
Create "Bauhaus Balance," a self-contained interactive mobile-sculpture toy in the spirit of a 1920s Bauhaus poster. Use warm paper, hard black, vermilion, cobalt and sunflower yellow. Compose an asymmetric constructivist layout with a huge "Bauhaus Balance" headline, a grid, an oversized yellow disc bleeding off the page, and a working two-arm mobile over a geometric fulcrum. The beam tilts realistically as weighted shapes (circle, square, triangle, half-circle) are added to left or right. Include live Left / Right mass totals, a torque angle readout, a reset control, and a poetic status. Each added shape hangs from a string and stacks geometrically below. All styling, motion and logic must be inline and self-contained; no external assets, fonts or libraries. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A constructivist playroom where the visitor composes a physical mobile. Primary-colored shapes, crisp black geometry and a gridded poster field make the balance experiment feel like a museum artifact that responds to touch.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Interactive CSS transform beam with physical tilt driven by added mass
- Geometric shape families with hard shadows and hanging-string construction
- Constructivist grid, oversized color fields and Swiss-poster typography
- Live numeric readouts and equilibrium state messaging
- Responsive recomposition that keeps the mobile playable on small screens

INTERACTION MODEL:
Add circles, squares, triangles or half-circles to either side using the labeled controls. The beam smoothly tips toward the heavier side; readouts show the mass on each side and the resulting angle. Press Reset to clear the composition and return to equilibrium.
