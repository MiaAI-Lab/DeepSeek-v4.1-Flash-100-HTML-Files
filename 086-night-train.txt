TITLE: The Night Train — Three Sleepers, One Long Corridor

PROMPT:
Create "The Night Train," a self-contained sleeper-train booking page. Use midnight navy, steel blue, warm cream, brass and a claret accent. Compose a three-column coach sheet: an editorial "The night train" title and three departure buttons (Vela to Trieste, Vienna, Prague); a carriage diagram with eight compartments along a corridor, each holding a lower and upper berth, with sold berths marked, available berths selectable and a selected berth highlighted in brass; and a journey panel with route, times, three travel classes (reclining, 2-berth, single), berth, fare, breakfast, total, a booking button and a ticket stub. Selecting a service or class should change route, times, fares and breakfast; selecting a berth should update the total; booking should issue a reference and lock the ticket. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A booking page that feels like the train itself: a long corridor, small compartments and the particular seriousness of a sleeper ticket. The carriage diagram is the interaction and the ticket stub is the payoff.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- CSS carriage diagram with compartments and corridor
- Berth selection with sold, available and selected states
- Service and class models driving route, fare and breakfast
- Ticket stub with generated reference
- Midnight, brass and cream railway palette

INTERACTION MODEL:
Choose a departure and a class, then select an available lower or upper berth in any compartment. Fare, berth, class and total update; press Book berth to issue a ticket and lock the booking. Sold berths cannot be selected.
