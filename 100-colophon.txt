TITLE: One Hundred — Colophon and Index

PROMPT:
Create "One Hundred," a self-contained closing colophon and index for a collection of 100 standalone HTML pieces. Use near-black ink, warm cream, antique gold and a rust accent. Compose a three-column closing sheet: an editorial "One hundred" title with a statement of method and counts of 100 pages, 100 prompts, 200 files and zero dependencies; a 10 × 10 index grid numbered 001–100 with short titles, hover and click selection; and a selected-piece panel with number, title, description, matching HTML and TXT filenames, a rules table (inline CSS and JS, no external assets, system fonts, no network, responsive) and a closing line. Hovering a cell should preview it; clicking should pin it and mark the cell. No external assets, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
The collection turned to face itself. The index is the illustration, the rules table is the argument, and the last page is deliberately quieter than the hundred before it.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- 10 × 10 generated index grid with numbers and short titles
- Hover preview and click-to-pin selection
- Filename derivation and per-piece descriptions
- Colophon rules table and counts
- Ink, cream and gold closing palette

INTERACTION MODEL:
Hover any cell to preview its number, title, description and filenames in the side panel. Click a cell to pin it and mark it with a gold border. The grid is complete at 100 and the page itself is piece 100.
