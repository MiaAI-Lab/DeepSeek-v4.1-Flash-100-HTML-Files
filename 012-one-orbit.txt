TITLE: One Orbit — A Game of Small Adjustments

PROMPT:
Create "One Orbit," a self-contained one-button arcade experience with the visual language of a premium Swiss poster. Use bone white, black ink and a single vivid vermilion. The centerpiece is a large generative orbital field: a black sun, three finely drawn lanes, a red satellite fixed at the top, and dark obstacles moving around the circles toward the player. Build a bold asymmetrical interface around it with huge "One orbit" type, a brief, live Score and Best counters, and an overlay invitation to begin. Clicking, tapping, Space or Enter shifts the satellite to the next orbit lane; avoiding each obstacle scores a pass. Collision ends the orbit and opens a graceful repeat state. Add optional synthesized Web Audio blips, accessible controls and responsive mobile layout. All inline; no external assets, libraries, fonts or network calls. Valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A minimalist radial arcade that looks like a design biennial poster and plays like a reflex ritual. Hard black geometry, one vermilion satellite and a bone-white field create clarity, while every successful pass leaves a small burst of celebratory particles.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas-drawn concentric orbital lanes, sun disc and moving geometric obstacles
- Asymmetric oversized grotesk typography and instrument-panel microcopy
- Particle bursts on success and a delicate pulsing satellite
- Optional procedural Web Audio feedback
- Responsive scaling with a composed mobile HUD

INTERACTION MODEL:
Click anywhere, tap, or press Space / Enter to hop one lane outward; the satellite cycles through three orbits. Avoid the black bars as they sweep around the sun. Score increases for every safe pass, Best is retained for the session, and pressing the start button after a collision resets the orbit.
