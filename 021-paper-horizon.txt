TITLE: Paper Horizon — A Landscape Cut From Light

PROMPT:
Create "Paper Horizon," a self-contained interactive cut-paper landscape in a collector's print frame. Use warm paper, dusty peach, sage, forest green, and a single apricot sun. Build a layered inline-SVG valley with seven cut-paper depth planes, each casting a fine shadow and edged with a pale cut line. Include a slightly rotated paper card, printed scene labels, elevation metadata, and a side index of four light studies: Dawn, Noon, Dusk and Night. Moving the pointer across the scene should parallax each paper layer at a different depth; choosing a light study changes sky gradients, mountain colors and the position and size of the sun. Clicking the scene cycles through the light studies. Everything inline and offline; no external images, fonts or libraries. Responsive, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A tactile paper diorama that turns slowly with the viewer's movement. The print-like frame, layered silhouettes and shifting hours make the landscape feel handmade, quiet and alive.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Layered inline SVG paper scenery with drop shadows and cut-edge highlights
- Pointer parallax mapped to per-layer depth values
- CSS custom-property palette transitions for four times of day
- Paper grain, print registration marks and editorial metadata
- Responsive scene framing and mobile light controls

INTERACTION MODEL:
Move across the scene to shift seven paper layers. Click the scene or use the side index to move between Dawn, Noon, Dusk and Night; the sun moves, the palette changes and the descriptive labels update. Click the cut-note to give the print a small physical tilt.
