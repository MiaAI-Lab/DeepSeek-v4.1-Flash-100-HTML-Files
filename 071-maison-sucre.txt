TITLE
Maison Sucré — The Sweet Course

PROMPT
Create an elegant dessert menu page for a fictional restaurant, Maison Sucré. Deep forest-green room, cream menu card, antique-gold rules and serif typography. The layout is a two-panel menu: on the left, the heading "The sweet course.", tabs for Desserts and Petits Fours, and a list of four items with descriptions, dotted leaders and prices; on the right, a detail panel showing the selected dessert as a refined SVG illustration on a plate, its name, a longer description, the recommended pairing and a "Choose this" action. Include a restaurant masthead, service hours, a gold seal and a footer. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS/SVG, no external images or fonts.

DESIGN INTENT
The last page of a tasting menu: rich, quiet and confident. The deep green makes cream and gold feel expensive, and the illustrations are simple enough to read as plate presentations rather than cartoons. Typography is a high-contrast serif with tiny tracked capitals, and every rule is a considered line, not a box.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
- Split menu card with a cream paper panel and a dark presentation panel.
- Four hand-drawn SVG desserts on plates: berry tart, chocolate sphere, soufflé and lemon tart.
- Dotted leader lines between dessert names and prices.
- Tab switching between desserts and petits fours with a short crossfade.
- Gold foil seal, hairline rules and a subtle paper texture.
- Serif display type, italic descriptions and monospaced service data.
- Responsive stack: detail appears below the menu list on mobile.

INTERACTION MODEL
Switching tabs changes the menu list. Selecting any dessert updates the illustration, description, pairing and price in the right panel. The "Pairing" control cycles three suggested drinks; "Choose this" confirms the selection with a short line. All menu items are keyboard-accessible buttons; reduced-motion removes the crossfade only.
