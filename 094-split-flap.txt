TITLE
Split-Flap — A Station Clock

PROMPT
Create a live split-flap station clock. A dark board with six cream flip cards shows hours, minutes and seconds in a monospaced face, separated by blinking colons, with a destination plate above reading the selected city. A control bar offers four cities (Local, Helsinki, Tokyo, New York), a 12/24-hour toggle, a "Flip all" test button and the full date. Every second the relevant cards flip with a mechanical rotation; changing city flips the whole board. Include station-hall ambience, departure-board typography and a footer. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS, no external images or fonts.

DESIGN INTENT
The pleasure of an airport board in a quiet station: cream cards, dark gaps, the little mechanical clack implied by motion. This is a clock you can watch, not a dashboard. The board is the hero, centred and large, with the controls treated as a station timetable strip underneath.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
- Six split-flap cards with a printed hinge line, subtle side screws and warm cream stock.
- 3D rotateX flip animation on digit change, with the value swapped mid-turn.
- Live time in four time zones using Intl.DateTimeFormat; 12/24-hour support.
- Blinking colon separators and a city/destination plate.
- Charcoal station background with panel lines and a warm overhead glow.
- Responsive card sizing that keeps all six digits on one row.
- Reduced-motion swaps digits without the flip.

INTERACTION MODEL
The clock runs automatically. City buttons retune all six digits to Local, Helsinki, Tokyo or New York and update the plate and UTC offset. The 12/24 toggle reformats hours and shows or hides the AM/PM badge. "Flip all" plays the flip animation across the board. Buttons expose pressed states, and the full date is always visible.
