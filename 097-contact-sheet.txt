TITLE: Contact Sheet 097 — Twelve Frames from an Unfinished Film

PROMPT:
Create "Contact Sheet 097," a self-contained darkroom contact sheet for a fictional unfinished film. Use near-black darkroom tones, warm paper, amber and faded red. Compose a three-column sheet: an editorial "Contact sheet" title with film metadata; a 4 × 3 contact sheet of twelve small frames, each an abstract inline-SVG still using a limited landscape palette, with frame numbers and a selected amber border; and a viewer panel with an enlarged frame, a director's margin note, frame number and timecode. Clicking a frame should enlarge it and replace the caption, note and timecode. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A roll of film as an object, not a gallery. The frames are small, imperfect and suggestive, and the margin notes make the unfinished film feel more real than any completed synopsis would.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Twelve procedural inline-SVG film stills with restrained palettes
- Contact-sheet grid with frame numbering and selection
- Enlarged viewer with director's annotations
- Darkroom black, amber and paper palette
- Film-strip and exposure metadata typography

INTERACTION MODEL:
Click any frame to enlarge it in the viewer and read its caption, margin note, frame number and timecode. The selected frame is outlined in amber. The sheet is otherwise a still archive.
