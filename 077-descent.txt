TITLE: The Descent — Eleven Thousand Metres of Water

PROMPT:
Create "The Descent," a self-contained deep-sea survey that moves from the surface to the hadal zone. Use a vertical gradient from sea blue through twilight navy to near-black, with bioluminescent cyan, soft pink and pale cream. Compose a dive screen: a left column with an editorial "Eleven kilometres" title and five zone buttons; a central water column with faint light rays, depth rules every kilometre, and seven drawn species positioned at their true depths (moon jelly, lanternfish, vampire squid, anglerfish, giant squid, grenadier, snailfish); a right panel with the selected species, Latin name, note, depth range, size, pressure and light; and a bottom depth slider with live depth, pressure and temperature. Moving the slider should slide species and depth rules through the column, change the water gradient and zone label, and update readouts. Clicking a species should select it. No external assets, images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
A vertical journey rather than a page. The creatures are simple drawn silhouettes, but placing them at their real depths gives the scroll a physical logic, and the changing water colour does the emotional work.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Inline-SVG species silhouettes with bioluminescent accents
- Depth-mapped positioning of creatures and kilometre rules
- Zone-based background gradients and label changes
- Live pressure and temperature computation from depth
- Light-ray overlay that fades into the abyss

INTERACTION MODEL:
Drag the depth slider from 0 to 11,000 m. The column moves past the species, the zone and water colour change, and pressure and temperature update. Click a zone button to jump there, or click any creature to read its species card.
