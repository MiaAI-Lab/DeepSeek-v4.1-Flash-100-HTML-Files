TITLE
Aurora Veil — Atmospheric Light Studio

PROMPT
Design a single-page site for a fictional "atmospheric light studio" called Aurora Veil. The feeling is cold northern light trapped inside frosted glass: a near-black midnight background, enormous soft aurora ribbons of teal, violet and rose drifting behind a centered hero, and a floating glass instrument panel that reads the "current atmosphere". Use an oversized editorial sans headline ("Light, suspended.") with the final word in an elegant italic serif, tight and cinematic. Add a slim glass navigation pill, a live clock and aurora index inside the panel, three "study" cards below the fold, and a slow ticker of poetic phrases at the bottom. Everything must feel expensive, quiet and luminous — generous negative space, hairline borders, inner highlights, film grain, no clutter. Colour palette: #05070f ink, #5ef2d6 teal, #8c7bff violet, #ff7ac8 rose, #eef2ff cream. Type: system grotesque with Palatino/Georgia serif italic accents. Animations should be slow (12–24s drift), never bouncy. Fully responsive, single self-contained HTML file, inline CSS and JS, no external assets.

DESIGN INTENT
A premium glassmorphism hero that behaves like a piece of atmospheric instrumentation. The aurora is the only loud element; every interface surface is thin, translucent and precise, so the colour appears to be sampled from the sky rather than painted onto the page. The composition is deliberately asymmetric — a large editorial statement on the left balanced by a small, dense instrument card on the right — with the lower third opening into three quiet study cards.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
- Three blurred, screen-blended gradient blobs animated on independent slow drift paths.
- Layered radial gradients and a vignette for depth; fixed film-grain overlay generated with an inline SVG feTurbulence data URI.
- Frosted-glass surfaces using backdrop-filter, 1px translucent borders, inset top highlights and soft layered shadows.
- Conic-gradient orb rotating inside the instrument card, masked with a radial fade.
- Fluid clamp() typography with a serif-italic accent and gradient text fill.
- Hairline grid lines, micro labels in tracked uppercase, animated spectrum bars.
- IntersectionObserver reveal transitions and a reduced-motion fallback.

INTERACTION MODEL
Moving the pointer shifts the aurora layers at different depths and moves a faint radial glow with the cursor. Hovering or tilting over the atmosphere card produces a subtle 3D tilt; the card returns softly on leave. The live clock and aurora index update every second. Study cards lift and reveal a line of copy on hover. Keyboard users get visible focus rings, and all motion is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
