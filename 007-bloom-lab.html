Generative botany / no. 07

Grow something
improbable.

A small field that exists only while you are looking at it. Plant a seed by clicking the paper; the lab will choose the stem, the leaves and the number of petals. Nothing here is rare except your attention.

MethodEach specimen is generated from three decisions: a position, a petal count and a palette. The rest is wind.
ObservationFlowers lean toward the nearest edge of the frame, as if the paper were a window left open.
ConservationNothing is saved, nothing is sent. Close the tab and the field returns to being a field.
