TITLE: Midnight Observatory — An Atlas of Borrowed Light

PROMPT:
Create "Midnight Observatory," a self-contained interactive celestial atlas with a museum-archival atmosphere. Use deep navy, warm ivory, dusty gold, ice blue and a rust star accent. Compose a three-column layout with an editorial serif "Midnight observatory" headline and field coordinates, a large circular star chart in the center, and a constellation index with lore on the right. The chart should be drawn in Canvas with subtle star scatter, month markings, concentric celestial rings, and four hand-plotted constellations (Orion, Cassiopeia, Cygnus, Lyra). Drag the chart to rotate the sky; selecting a constellation highlights its lines and updates the Latin name, story and astronomical note. Provide Lines and Labels toggles. All CSS, JS and artwork inline; no external images, fonts, libraries or network calls. Responsive, offline, valid HTML5.

DESCRIPTION:
An observatory plate for the browser age: quiet, exact and full of old light. Gold celestial geometry and ivory serif typography float in a deep indigo field, while the chart can be turned by hand like a physical planisphere.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas star chart with procedurally scattered stars and precise concentric rings
- Rotatable celestial projection with pointer drag
- Constellation line-work and active-label highlighting
- Hand-drawn typographic composition with archival microdata
- Grain overlay and subtle atmospheric light

INTERACTION MODEL:
Drag anywhere on the star chart to turn the sky. Choose Orion, Cassiopeia, Cygnus or Lyra from the index to highlight its stars, draw its connective lines and reveal a short myth with distance notes. Lines and Labels toggles let the viewer simplify the chart.
